About Greenwood 100 Inc.

Building bridges between communities, one neighborhood at a time.

Our Story

Founded in Jacksonville, Florida, Greenwood 100 Inc. began as a grassroots initiative to strengthen local communities through economic empowerment and neighborhood engagement.

What started as a small directory of local businesses has grown into a comprehensive platform connecting residents, entrepreneurs, investors, and community organizations across multiple cities.

Today, we're expanding nationwide and worldwide, bringing our proven model of community growth to neighborhoods everywhere.

Community Land Trust

Building generational wealth through collectively owned community assets

The Greenwood 100 Community Land Trust allows members to pool resources and collectively acquire properties that remain permanently affordable and community-controlled.

How It Works

1

Members Contribute

Members contribute to the Land Trust fund at any level they are able to participate

2

Trust Acquires Properties

The Trust acquires properties on behalf of the community through collective purchasing power

3

Permanent Community Ownership

Properties remain in community ownership permanently — residents pay affordable rent/lease rates forever
